## **App Concept: “QuipNote”**
### **Purpose**
- Give everyone a simple, private way to record and verify their helpful actions or gestures—starting from small, everyday moments.
- No invasive tracking; just proof of positive interaction, using device haptics (touch/press) as confirmation.
### **Core Features**
1. **Quick Entry**
   - One-tap or swipe to log a gesture or helpful action (e.g., “Helped someone,” “Shared info,” “Contributed to a project”).
   - Optional short note or emoji to describe the action.
2. **Proof of Interaction**
   - Uses the phone’s haptic feedback (vibration or tap) as a “stamp” for each entry—no GPS, no personal data, no area code tracking.
   - Entries are timestamped and stored securely on the device.
3. **Verification**
   - Users can “verify” each other’s actions in person (e.g., a friend taps their phone too), creating a simple, mutual validation.
   - Optionally, users can export a summary of their gestures as proof for jobs, community groups, or personal growth.
4. **Privacy First**
   - No location tracking, no personal data shared.
   - All notes stay on the device unless the user chooses to share or export them.
5. **Reminders & Reflection**
   - Gentle reminders to log daily gestures.
   - Weekly or monthly summaries to reflect on positive actions.
